Thesis Supervision & Opportunities

We supervise Bachelor's and Master's theses across sport psychology, cognitive movement science, and sports informatics.

Our lab provides an active environment for students looking to conduct rigorous quantitative research using modern empirical techniques, eye-tracking paradigms, and open-science protocols.

Primary Supervision Areas

  • Nonverbal Communication: Experimental designs examining body language and emotion perception in competitive settings.
  • Attentional Processes: Eye-tracking studies on visual search strategies and decision-making in sports.
  • High-Pressure Performance: Applied psychological paradigms measuring performance dynamics under pressure.
  • Open Science Practices: Projects focused on replication studies, preregistration, and transparent data analysis in sport science.

Supervision Guidelines & Process

1. Choosing a Topic

Students can propose their own research ideas aligning with our core research themes, or apply for ongoing empirical projects currently active in the institute.

2. Methodological Standards

We emphasize strong statistical foundations, preregistration of hypotheses, systematic data collection, and reproducible analyses using standard software tools (JASP, R, SPSS).

Application Procedure

If you are interested in writing your thesis or conducting a research internship, please submit an inquiry via email with the following attachments:

  • A current Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  • A short outline (1 page) of your research interests or specific thesis idea
  • An overview of completed coursework and current transcript of records
