    <h2>Thesis Supervision & Opportunities</h2>
    <p class="lead">
      We supervise Bachelor's and Master's theses across sport psychology, cognitive movement science, and sports informatics.
    </p>
    <p>
      Our lab provides an active environment for students looking to conduct rigorous quantitative research using modern empirical techniques, eye-tracking paradigms, and open-science protocols.
    </p>

    <div class="row pt-4 pb-4 align-items-center">
      <div class="col-sm-4 mb-3 mb-sm-0">
        <img src="/assets/img/prof_pic.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" alt="Student Research Lab">
      </div>
      <div class="col-sm-8">
        <h3>Primary Supervision Areas</h3>
        <ul>
          <li><strong>Nonverbal Communication:</strong> Experimental designs examining body language and emotion perception in competitive settings.</li>
          <li><strong>Attentional Processes:</strong> Eye-tracking studies on visual search strategies and decision-making in sports.</li>
          <li><strong>High-Pressure Performance:</strong> Applied psychological paradigms measuring performance dynamics under pressure.</li>
          <li><strong>Open Science Practices:</strong> Projects focused on replication studies, preregistration, and transparent data analysis in sport science.</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>

    <h2>Application Procedure</h2>
    <p>
      If you are interested in writing your thesis or conducting a research internship, please submit an inquiry via email with the following attachments:
    </p>
    <ul>
      <li>A current Curriculum Vitae (CV)</li>
      <li>A short outline (1 page) of your research interests or specific thesis idea</li>
      <li>An overview of completed coursework and current transcript of records</li>
